01 · Foundation

I'm Yun Yue Zhao — a Senior Consultant in Deloitte's Finance Transformation practice, engaged with a real estate asset management platform at a major global financial institution.

The trajectory wasn't accidental. I started at the University of Washington in biochemistry — a foundation that taught me to read complex systems patiently and to trust precise measurement over confident assertion. From there I pivoted into finance and accounting, earning a Master of Accountancy at the University of Denver and CPA licenses in California and Colorado — credentials chosen for the doors they open into analytical, consequential financial work.

02 · Audit

The next five years were spent in Big 4 audit — across investment funds, banks, and capital markets clients. At Plante Moran I served as an Audit Senior in the investment fund practice — engagements spanning real estate funds, private equity funds, and alternative investment vehicles. Working fluency in fund accounting, NAV preparation, waterfalls, and capital call mechanics was built up by auditing the books and records of dozens of fund vehicles. At PwC I moved into the Banking & Capital Markets practice in Denver, working with financial institutions across credit, real estate lending, and structured finance portfolios.

Big 4 audit teaches you what financial systems look like from the inside — every line item, every control, every place a number meets a judgment.

03 · MBA & Now

In 2023 I moved to Tokyo for an MBA at Waseda University, complemented by six months on the hedge fund desk at Asian Technology Advisors — research on 15+ global companies across semiconductors, technology, and automotive, supporting senior-analyst coverage decisions on portfolios ranging from $200M to $10B AUM. The buy-side seat teaches the inverse of the audit seat: not how financial systems work internally, but how markets price what those systems produce.

I joined Deloitte in December 2025 deliberately — to work directly alongside the people who run institutional real estate finance at a major global financial institution. The work bridges audit-grounded technical depth, post-MBA strategic framing, and AI-enabled finance modernization — applied to the operating model and reporting infrastructure of an institutional real estate platform.

I hold CPA licenses in California and Colorado, am pursuing CFA Level III, and work across English, Mandarin, and Japanese.
